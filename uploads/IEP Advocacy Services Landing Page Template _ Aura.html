About

A polished template for IEP advocacy and special education support services, ideal for consultants and advocates to promote offerings and book consultations.

Wynn Advocacy Landing Page Template

A refined, conversion-focused template for IEP advocates and special education consultants to showcase expertise, services, and client results.

Key Features

  • Hero section with clear positioning, trust stats, and primary consultation CTA
  • Services & pricing cards for consultations, document review, and meeting attendance
  • Methodology section to explain your unique process or framework
  • About & expertise areas to highlight credentials, specializations, and locations served
  • Testimonials & community blocks for social proof and Facebook group growth

Primary Use Case

Perfect for independent IEP advocates, special education consultants, and small family-advocacy practices who need a professional landing page template to attract parents, explain services, and drive bookings for free or paid consultations.
